COURSE OVERVIEW

Understand the Networks You Protect

Cybersecurity depends heavily on networking. Before a security professional can defend, monitor or test a network, they need to understand how devices discover each other, exchange packets, resolve names, establish connections and communicate with services.

Networking Fundamentals teaches these concepts from a security perspective. You'll examine the technologies behind everyday network communication while also learning why attackers and defenders care about protocols, ports, addressing, routing and traffic behaviour.

What You'll Learn

Understand network models and packet communication.
Understand IPv4 addressing and private/public addresses.
Understand CIDR notation and subnetting fundamentals.
Explain MAC addresses and ARP.
Compare TCP and UDP.
Understand ICMP and network diagnostics.
Understand routing and gateways.
Understand DNS and DHCP.
Understand HTTP and HTTPS traffic.
Analyze networking concepts from a cybersecurity perspective.

CYBERSECURITY PERSPECTIVE

Why Networking Matters in Security

Firewalls, vulnerability scanners, intrusion detection systems, SIEM platforms and penetration-testing tools all analyse network communication. Understanding the underlying protocols allows security professionals to distinguish normal traffic from suspicious behaviour.
