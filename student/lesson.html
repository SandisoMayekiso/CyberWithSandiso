CYBERSECURITY FUNDAMENTALS

What Is Cybersecurity?

Understanding the purpose of cybersecurity and why it matters.

01

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, systems and information from unauthorized access, misuse, disruption, modification or destruction.

Modern organizations depend heavily on technology. Businesses store customer information, process financial transactions, communicate through digital systems and operate critical infrastructure using computers and networks.

This dependence creates opportunities for attackers. Cybersecurity exists to reduce those risks and help organizations protect the systems and information they depend on.

02

Why Does Cybersecurity Matter?

A successful cyberattack can have consequences far beyond a single compromised computer. Organizations can experience financial loss, operational disruption, reputational damage and exposure of sensitive information.

Protect Information

Prevent unauthorized people from accessing sensitive or confidential information.

Protect Systems

Keep computers, applications and infrastructure secure and reliable.

Protect Organizations

Reduce operational, financial and reputational risks.

03

The Three Core Security Objectives

One of the most important concepts in cybersecurity is the CIA triad. CIA stands for Confidentiality, Integrity and Availability.

C

Confidentiality

Information should only be accessible to authorized individuals and systems.

Example: Access controls
I

Integrity

Information should remain accurate, trustworthy and protected from unauthorized modification.

Example: File integrity monitoring
A

Availability

Systems and information should be accessible when authorized users need them.

Example: Backups and redundancy
04

What Does Cybersecurity Protect?

Cybersecurity covers many different technologies and environments.

  • Computers and endpoints
  • Networks and network infrastructure
  • Web and mobile applications
  • Cloud environments
  • Databases and information systems
  • User identities and accounts
05

Developing a Security Mindset

Cybersecurity is not simply about installing security software. It involves understanding how systems work, identifying weaknesses, evaluating risk and implementing appropriate protections.

Security mindset

When examining a system, ask: What are we protecting? What could go wrong? Who could cause the problem? What would happen if the system were compromised? How can we reduce the risk?

06

Key Takeaways

  • Cybersecurity protects systems, networks and information.
  • Cybersecurity helps reduce the impact of cyber threats and security risks.
  • Confidentiality protects information from unauthorized access.
  • Integrity protects information from unauthorized modification.
  • Availability ensures systems and information remain accessible when needed.
