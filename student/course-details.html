COURSE OVERVIEW

Build Your Cybersecurity Foundation

Cybersecurity Fundamentals introduces the core principles used to protect information, systems, networks and users.

You'll learn how security professionals identify assets, understand threats, evaluate vulnerabilities, manage risk and apply appropriate security controls.

The course combines structured lessons, practical exercises, knowledge checks and assessments to help you develop a strong foundation before progressing into networking, Linux, ethical hacking and application security.

What You'll Learn

  • Understand the purpose and principles of cybersecurity.
  • Explain the CIA triad and fundamental security objectives.
  • Identify common cyber threats and threat actors.
  • Understand vulnerabilities, exploits and security risks.
  • Explain common cybersecurity controls and defensive strategies.
  • Understand authentication, authorization and access control.
  • Recognize common attack techniques and their defensive implications.
  • Understand basic security monitoring and incident response concepts.
  • Apply cybersecurity concepts to practical scenarios.
  • Develop a foundation for further cybersecurity study.
