FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

CWS Academy FAQ

A few things to know before you start learning.

Is CWS Academy free?

Yes. CWS Academy includes free learning paths covering cybersecurity, networking, Linux, Bash, Python and Active Directory fundamentals.

Do I need cybersecurity experience?

No. Beginners can start with Cybersecurity Fundamentals before progressing into networking, Linux and intermediate technical courses.

Do I need Kali Linux?

Not for every course. Individual courses explain which operating systems, virtual machines or tools are useful for their practical exercises.

Are the labs legal?

CWS Academy practical exercises are intended for systems you own or environments where you have explicit authorization to test. Security testing should never be performed against unauthorized systems.

Can I track my progress?

Yes. Registered students can track course and lesson progress through their CWS Academy student area.

When will CWS Academy Pro launch?

Pro learning paths are currently being developed and remain disabled while advanced course content, labs and access controls are completed.
