Privacy Policy

This policy explains how CWS Academy handles personal information when you use the public website or learning platform.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. Information we may process

CWS Academy may process information such as your name, email address, authentication details, course progress, assessment results, lab progress, certificate and credential records, subscription or entitlement status, support messages, and technical information needed to operate and secure the platform.

2. Why we process information

Information is used to provide accounts, deliver learning content, save progress, administer assessments and labs, issue and verify eligible credentials, provide support, maintain security, manage subscriptions, improve CWS Academy and comply with applicable obligations.

3. Service providers

CWS Academy currently uses Firebase for platform functions such as authentication and database storage. Additional providers may be used for payments, video delivery, analytics, hosting or other functions. Their own privacy practices may also apply.

4. Public credentials

Eligible CWS credentials may have a public verification record so employers or recruiters can confirm a credential ID. Public verification should expose only information needed for verification rather than private learning records.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Essential storage supports core functions. Optional analytics, embedded media and marketing technologies are controlled through the CWS cookie-preference interface.

Read the Cookie Policy.

6. Security and retention

CWS Academy uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect platform information and should retain information only as long as reasonably required for platform operation, credentials, security, support or applicable obligations.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, objection, consent withdrawal and complaints concerning your personal information. Requests can be made through the CWS Academy contact channel.

8. Contact and updates

Questions can be submitted through the Contact page. This policy may be updated as CWS Academy adds payments, video hosting, analytics or other functionality.
