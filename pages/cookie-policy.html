Cookie Policy

This page explains how CWS Academy uses cookies and browser storage and how you can control optional technologies.

Last updated: 21 August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Websites may also use technologies such as local storage for similar functional purposes.

2. CWS Academy categories

CategoryPurposeDefault
EssentialAuthentication, security, navigation and saved privacy preferences.Always enabled
AnalyticsOptional usage measurement to improve the platform.Off until allowed
Embedded MediaThird-party media such as YouTube lesson videos.Off until allowed
MarketingOptional advertising, campaign measurement or remarketing if introduced later.Off until allowed

3. Your choices

You can accept all optional categories, use only essential technologies, or choose categories individually. Your decision is stored in your browser.

4. YouTube lessons

When YouTube lessons are introduced, CWS Academy intends to use privacy-enhanced embedding where practical and to avoid loading the embed until Media consent has been granted.

5. Changing your decision

You may reopen Cookie Settings at any time and change your optional preferences. Some third-party information already stored by another service may need to be controlled through that provider or your browser.

6. Contact

Questions about cookies or privacy can be submitted through the Contact page.
